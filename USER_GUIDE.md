# Hok6 User Guide

*Where you learn, study, and imitate.*

How to use Hok6: Trace, Write and Learn once it's installed. Parts 1–3 and 6–7 work for a book in any language;
parts 4 and 5 are extra practice for languages written with characters (Cantonese, Mandarin, Japanese, Korean). To
install it and set up the tablet, see [Install](README.md#install) in the README.

Hok6 has been tested only on a **Samsung Galaxy Tab S7 FE** (Android 14), a **Razer Edge** (Android 12, which
uses the phone layout) and a **Samsung Galaxy S25 Ultra** (Android 17). It should work on other devices with
Android 8.0 or newer, but they haven't been tried.

## 1. Put your book on the device

Copy your book to the device (e.g. into **Download**) as one folder, with a sub-folder for each chapter:

```
My Book/
├── Chapter 1/
│   ├── Homework.pdf
│   ├── Vocabulary.docx
│   └── Lesson 1.mp3
├── Chapter 2/
│   └── …
└── Notes.pdf          ← files here show as "Files in book folder"
```

Folders can hold more folders, so one folder can hold several books, each with chapter folders or not:

```
My Library/
├── Book 1/
│   ├── Chapter 1/
│   └── Chapter 2/
└── Book 2/            ← a book without chapters: its files are right here
    └── Lesson 1.pdf
```

PDFs, Word documents (`.docx`) and audio (mp3, m4a, wav, …) open in the app; other files open in another app.
Chapters are sorted the way you'd expect, so "Chapter 2" comes before "Chapter 10".

On a Samsung tablet the book can be on the microSD card: when choosing the book folder, tap ☰ in the folder picker
and pick the SD card.

## 2. Open your book

1. Open **Hok6** and tap **Choose book folder**. Pick your book folder, tap **Use this folder**, then **Allow**.
   Hok6 remembers it; **Change book folder** (folder icon) picks another one. Before Android's folder screen opens,
   Hok6 shows your current folder with **Cancel**; on Android's screen, press **Back** until you're back in Hok6 to
   leave without choosing (each press first goes up a folder).
2. Tap a folder card. A folder with more folders in it (e.g. a book with chapters) opens as cards again, with the
   folders above it shown under the title; **Back** goes up a level. A folder of files opens its file list; tap a file
   to open it full screen, and **Back** returns to the list. A folder with both shows its files as a
   **Files in this folder** card.
3. Tap ☆ on a folder card to make it a favourite: favourites are listed first (★), handy when there are many folders.
   They're kept in the backup with the rest of your work.
4. Tap an audio file to play the folder's recordings, with speed and repeat controls. Playback carries on with the
   screen off, with controls on the lock screen.

## 3. Do the homework on the page

- **Write** straight on a PDF or Word page with a finger or stylus. Scroll and zoom with two fingers.
- **Stylus or finger:** on a device that takes a stylus, the title bar has a toggle. **✍ Stylus**: only the stylus
  writes and fingers scroll, so your hand can rest on the screen. **☝ Finger**: fingers write too (lost the stylus,
  or a child using a finger). The first time you use a stylus Hok6 switches to **✍ Stylus**; after that it remembers
  your choice, on homework pages and in Practice & Quiz alike. Devices without a stylus have no toggle: fingers
  always write.
- The pen, its thickness and the eraser are in a small bar on the right side of the page: tap the pen to open the
  colours, and the line under it for the thickness (the line shows how thick the pen is). After erasing, one tap on the
  pen goes back to writing with the same colour. Zoom with − / + in the title bar, or pinch.
- **Translate to English** (the 文A button in the title bar) opens a panel at the bottom of the page with the page's
  text in English: the PDF page in the middle of the screen, following as you scroll, or the Word file. Scanned pages
  are read on the device first. The translation is made on the device, from the language the page is written in. The
  first time, it downloads that language's translation model from Google, which needs internet; after that it works
  offline. Tap ✕ or Back to close the panel.
- **Clear** erases the tracing on one page. It asks first, and asks which page when two pages with tracing are on
  screen. The message that appears afterwards offers **Undo**.
- Tap the page number at the bottom (e.g. **2 / 5**) to go to another page. Hok6 remembers the page you were on.
- Your writing is saved automatically for each page, and stays exactly where you wrote it when you zoom or turn the
  tablet.
- When it's finished, ⋮ → **Save a copy with my tracing…** saves a new PDF with your writing on it, named e.g.
  `completed_Homework.pdf`, in the same folder. The original isn't changed; Hok6 warns you before replacing a file.

Word documents are shown like a page as wide as the screen's narrow side, keeping headings, bold and italic, tables
and pinyin/jyutping above characters. Zooming doesn't re-wrap the lines, so tracing stays on its words.

## 4. Practice writing characters

- On a page, tap **Practice Selector**, then tap a character: Hok6 shows the characters on **that page** (in a Word
  document, that paragraph), not the whole file. Pick the ones you want and tap **Practice (n)** to get a
  worksheet with stroke order, a row to trace and rows to write in. Tap a pen to go back to writing on the page.
  Scanned pages (pictures with no text) are read on the device; check the characters it found are right. If you're
  learning more than one language, the **🌐** button before **Practice Selector** shows the language you're practising
  and switches it. If a scanned page looks like another language, Hok6 asks whether to use it for your language
  anyway or switch (a page that's nearly all kanji can look Chinese).
- Or tap **Practice & Quiz** on the main screen. Its **✏️ Practice writing** tab makes worksheets (the quiz has its
  own tab). Its heading names the language you're practising (e.g. **New Korean worksheet**). It opens on **⌨ Type**:
  type characters, words or English (e.g. *thank you*; English shows words in the language you're practising right
  under the box; tap one to use it), or switch to **✍ Draw** to write on the pad and
  tap the right one; it's added to **To practice**. **Squares: 1–4** above the pad sets how many characters it holds,
  so a whole word can be written at once, one character per square (remembered). The first time you draw in a language, tap
  **⬇ Download handwriting** under the pad to get its recognition (once; then it works offline). Then tap **Create
  worksheet**. Box size, guide lines and the rest are under **Worksheet options** (kept from your last worksheet).
  Words you've practiced are in **History** below, one tap from a fresh worksheet.
- On a worksheet, tap a stroke-order square to watch the strokes and try **✍ Write it** (red is always the stroke
  being written; **Radical** shows the character's radical, the part dictionaries sort it under, in green); tap 🔊 to hear the word,
  and the speed button next to it for 1×, 0.75× or 0.5×. Tone names are shown with the reading (e.g. nei5 · tone 5,
  low rising).
- The worksheet's title bar has the same writing tools as a homework page: tap the pen for the colours (after erasing,
  one tap goes back to the pen), the line for the thickness,
  and **Clear** (with Undo in the message). **Export** prints it or shares it as a PDF (US Letter), blank or with your writing. It has the same
  **✍ Stylus / ☝ Finger** toggle (under **⋮** on phones), and a finger tap on a stroke-order square still opens it in
  stylus mode. **⋮** holds **Zoom in / out** and **Fit to screen**, which sets the zoom back
  to its starting size (the menu stays open while you zoom; tap outside it to close).
- Your writing on a worksheet is kept for each word, so it's still there when you practice that word again.
- **History** lists the words and characters you've practiced in the language you're practising now (with quiz
  results) and your bookmarks, each one tap away from a fresh worksheet. **📝** on a row adds it to the quiz (tap again
  to take it out). **✕** on a row removes it (with Undo); **Clear history** removes this language's history, or only
  its words or characters when **Words** or **Characters** is picked (**Clear what's shown**). Bookmarks and writing
  are kept.
- If you're learning more than one language (ticked in ⋮ → **Settings** → **Languages**: **Cantonese**,
  **Mandarin**, **Japanese**, **Korean**), switch between them at the top. Languages are never mixed: History, the
  quiz and the English look-up show only the language you're practising, alongside English. Words typed (or picked on
  a page) that aren't in that language, such as 谢 while practising Korean, are left out, and the message says how
  many. Recognition works in that language alone too: **✍ Draw** offers only its words, **Practice Selector** lists
  only its characters, and a scanned page in another language isn't offered at all (the message says so). Typing kana or hangul
  switches to Japanese or Korean by itself. On a worksheet, the switch also writes each word the way that language
  does (学校 becomes 學校 in Cantonese and 학교 in Korean), with its own stroke order; a word that language doesn't
  have is left out (the message says how many), and switching back brings it back. Stroke order only ever comes from
  the language's own data, never another language's: Korean has stroke order for hangul and for the hanja learned
  first, so most other hanja (and a few rare characters in other languages) are shown without it, and the worksheet
  says so. For Cantonese, **Stroke order: Hong Kong / Mainland China** on the **✏️ Practice writing** tab (also in
  ⋮ → **Settings** → **Languages**) chooses whose order to follow. **Hong Kong** (the default) follows Hong Kong's
  order, checked against the Education Bureau's Lexical Lists for Chinese Learning in Hong Kong; characters it
  couldn't be matched for keep mainland China's (about one in five of the list). The worksheet says which characters differ from mainland China's. Mandarin always
  follows mainland China's order, its own standard. Follow your textbook or teacher where they disagree.

On a phone, worksheets show one character at a time with big squares.

## 5. Quiz yourself

1. On a page, tap **Practice Selector**, tap a character, choose the ones to learn and tap **Add to quiz**. Or, in
   **Practice & Quiz**, tap **📝** next to a word in History or Bookmarks.
2. Open **Practice & Quiz** and tap the **📝 Quiz** tab (it shows how many are in the quiz):
   - **Flash cards**: choose Word → meaning, English → word or Sound → word, tap **Start flash cards**, then
     **Show answer** and mark **Knew it** or **Again**.
   - **Written quiz**: choose the prompt (English meaning, the character, which can hide after a few seconds,
     or the pronunciation), tap **Start written quiz** and write each character in the box. It's checked stroke by
     stroke, with a hint after 3 misses; **Show me** shows how it's written.
3. Missed ones come back at the end of the round, and you can quiz the missed ones again.

## 6. Keep your files tidy and your work safe

- **Move files:** in a folder's file list, long-press a file (or ⋮ → **Move files…**), select files, tap **Move to…**
  and pick any folder of the book (shown with its path, e.g. *Book 1 › Chapter 2*, favourites first) or **＋ New
  folder**, which is made next to the current folder. Tracing and the last page read move with the files.
  ⋮ → **New folder…** on the folder cards makes a folder there.
  The first time, Hok6 asks you to choose the book folder again and tap **Allow**, so it may change files.
- **Backup:** Hok6 keeps a backup of your writing, tracing, history, bookmarks, favourites and quiz in your book
  folder, as `Hok6 backup.json`, and updates it each time you leave a screen after changing something. It stays on the
  tablet if Hok6 is removed. After reinstalling, choose the same book folder and tap **Restore**.
  When you copy the book folder to a computer or another device, the backup goes with it.
- To save a copy somewhere else, main screen ⋮ → **Back up my work…**; **Restore my work…** brings back any backup
  file you pick. Restoring adds to what's there: history, bookmarks and the quiz are combined.
- Nothing goes to Google Drive, and no Google account is needed.

## 7. Settings

**About Hok6** (⋮ → **About Hok6**, at the bottom of the menu on the main screen, in Practice & Quiz and on a worksheet) shows the version, has
**Check for updates** (through Google Play, or GitHub for a copy installed from there), links to this guide, the
privacy policy and the source, and the device tips and credits.

Main screen ⋮ → **Settings**:

- **Languages**: tick the languages you're learning (Cantonese, Mandarin, Japanese, Korean, one or more; the welcome screen asks the
  first time). Hok6 gets the handwriting recognition and voice for each one ticked. Cantonese is written in traditional
  characters, so simplified ones (学, 谢) are left out of it; tick **Simplified characters too** under Cantonese to
  let them in. **Stroke order** under Cantonese is the same Hong Kong / Mainland China choice as on the Practice
  writing tab.
- **Downloads**: the handwriting models and voices for those languages, with **Download all**. Needed once, with
  internet; after that Hok6 works offline.
- **Voices**: which engine Hok6 speaks with: Google's (recommended; it has voices for all four) or the device's
  own. Changing it here doesn't change the device's voice setting for other apps. **Add voices…** opens
  Google's voice download; **Android voice settings…** opens the device's own.
- **Download reminders**: whether Hok6 offers a missing voice when it's needed. (Handwriting recognition never pops
  up: in **✍ Draw**, a **⬇ Download handwriting** button under the pad gets it.)
- **Light or dark**: *Same as the tablet* (the default), *Light* or *Dark*. Practice & Quiz follows it too.
- **Welcome screen**: shows the first-start screen again.

## Tips

- **Hand on the screen:** with **✍ Stylus** in the title bar, only the stylus writes; fingers scroll and zoom. Lost
  the stylus? Tap it to switch to **☝ Finger**.
- **Portrait or landscape:** turn the tablet to portrait for a full page, landscape for wider worksheets. Tracing
  stays in place either way.
- **No sound from 🔊:** without internet, a voice only works once it's downloaded. Connect to Wi-Fi, or ⋮ →
  **Settings** → **Voices** → **Add voices…**. See also *Voices* in the README's
  [device settings](README.md#device-settings).
- **Text size:** Hok6 follows Android's font size setting, and is already a step bigger on tablets.
