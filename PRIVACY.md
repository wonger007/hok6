# Hok6 privacy policy

*Last updated: 8 October 2026*

Hok6 is a study app for your own textbook. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and its developer receives none of your data.

## What stays on your tablet

Your book, your handwriting, tracing, notes, settings and favourites are kept on your device only, in Hok6's own storage and in the book folder you choose (including the `Hok6 backup.json` file there). Hok6 does not upload them anywhere. Android's cloud backup is turned off for Hok6.

## What Hok6 uses from Google

- **Handwriting and text recognition** use Google's on-device ML Kit. The first time you use handwriting, its recognition model is downloaded from Google. Recognition then happens on your tablet; your handwriting and pages are not sent anywhere. ML Kit's usage reporting to Google is switched off in Hok6.
- **Spoken words** use the text-to-speech voice installed on your tablet (usually Google's Speech Services). The voice is provided by that app under its own privacy policy.

## Sharing

If you share a page or file from Hok6, it goes only to the app you pick.

## Children

Hok6 is made for learners of all ages, including children. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included: there is no sign-up, Hok6 sends nothing about its users anywhere, and there are no ads and no in-app purchases. A child's writing and practice history stay on the device, and a parent can delete them at any time by clearing Hok6's data or uninstalling it (and deleting `Hok6 backup.json` from the book folder).
