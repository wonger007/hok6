# Hok6 privacy policy

*Last updated: 8 October 2026*

Hok6 is a study app for your own textbook. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics of its own, and its developer receives none of your data.

## What stays on your tablet

Your book, your handwriting, tracing, notes, settings and favourites are kept on your device only, in Hok6's own storage and in the book folder you choose (including the `Hok6 backup.json` file there). Hok6 does not upload them anywhere. Android's cloud backup is turned off for Hok6.

## What Hok6 uses from Google

- **Handwriting and text recognition** use Google's on-device ML Kit. The first time you use them, the recognition models are downloaded from Google. Recognition then happens on your tablet; your handwriting and pages are not sent to Google. ML Kit may send Google basic technical information (such as the device model, app version and how the recogniser performed) to keep it working and improve it. See [ML Kit's terms and privacy information](https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms).
- **Spoken words** use the text-to-speech voice installed on your tablet (usually Google's Speech Services). The voice is provided by that app under its own privacy policy.

## Sharing

If you share a page or file from Hok6, it goes only to the app you pick.

## Children

Hok6 is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone.
