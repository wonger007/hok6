# Test Hok6

1. **Join the testers group** (anyone can join): **[groups.google.com/g/hok6-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/hok6-testers)**
2. **Become a tester:** **[play.google.com/apps/testing/com.wonger.hok6](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.wonger.hok6)**
3. **Install:** **[Hok6 on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wonger.hok6)**

Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your device, and please keep Hok6 installed for two weeks.
The steps are explained below.

---

**Hok6** (學, *hok6*) is an Android app that turns your own textbook and worksheets (PDF or Word) into pages you can
write and trace on with a finger or stylus, in any language, and plays the lesson audio. For Chinese (Cantonese or
Mandarin) it adds stroke-order practice, worksheets and quizzes. It's in testing on Google Play, and you can help by
trying it.

You need an Android phone or tablet (Android 8.0 or newer) and a Google account.

## 1. Join the testers group

Open **[groups.google.com/g/hok6-testers](https://groups.google.com/g/hok6-testers)** and tap **Join group**.

Use the same Google account as the Play Store on your device. You won't get emails from the group.

## 2. Become a tester

Open **[play.google.com/apps/testing/com.wonger.hok6](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.wonger.hok6)** and tap
**Become a tester**.

If it says the app isn't available to you, wait a few minutes after joining the group and try again.

## 3. Install Hok6

Tap **download it on Google Play** on that page, or open
**[Hok6 on Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.wonger.hok6)**, and install it.
Updates come through the Play Store like any app.

## Please keep it for two weeks

Google needs at least 12 testers to stay in the test for **14 days in a row** before Hok6 can be released to
everyone, so please keep it installed (and stay in the group) for at least two weeks. Using it now and then helps too.

## Tell us what you think

Found a problem or have an idea? Open an issue at
**[github.com/wonger007/hok6/issues](https://github.com/wonger007/hok6/issues)**, or tell the person who sent you
here.

## Leaving the test

Open the [tester page](https://play.google.com/apps/testing/com.wonger.hok6) again and tap **Leave the program**, then
uninstall Hok6.
